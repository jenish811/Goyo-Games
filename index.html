04 — The studio

Who we are

GOYO Games is a small studio with a loud opinion: a game should feel inviting in the first five seconds and rewarding for the next five hundred hours.

We start every project with a paper prototype and one honest question — is this actually fun yet? Nothing gets art, audio or a roadmap until the answer is yes. That discipline is why our games look simple and play deep.

We're designers, engineers, artists and one very opinionated QA lead, spread across three time zones and united by a build channel that never sleeps.
